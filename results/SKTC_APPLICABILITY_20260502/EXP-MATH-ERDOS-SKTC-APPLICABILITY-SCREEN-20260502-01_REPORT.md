## Applicability Signature

A problem is SKTC-promising when it has:

1. an extremal functional or deficit,
2. a finite or compact-ish state space,
3. symmetry or invariance worth quotienting,
4. boundary/singularity/phase-transition behavior,
5. an operator, tensor, or transfer-matrix representation,
6. a feasible finite calibration ladder,
7. negative controls or neighboring problems for triangulation.
